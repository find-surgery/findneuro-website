The Method
FIND the driver network
Criticality is a per-contact score of how strongly each recording site drives the seizure network.
  • Delay-adjusted wavelet transfer entropy with phase slope index and phase-amplitude coupling, at 4 to 120 Hz, across pre-ictal, peri-ictal and post-ictal windows
  • Directional and delay-aware, so driving separates from propagating - which broadband, single-timescale methods cannot do
  • Ground truth is the treatment zone segmented from post-operative T1, not the annotated seizure onset zone

What We Do Not Claim
Limitations, from us rather than from you.
  • Retrospective. Whether it changes decisions in a live surgical conference is exactly the open question.
  • Penn contributed 78% of the validation cohort. Excluding it, d = 0.68 (CI 0.12-1.24) - suggestive that the effect is not driven by one site, but underpowered.
  • Mixed sEEG and ECoG. Criticality is not yet validated separately by recording type.
  • About half the training cohort was labelled with the annotated SOZ rather than a segmented treatment zone.
  • The 0.15 threshold was pre-locked. Adaptive thresholding is unstudied.
  • U.S. Level-4 centers only. Neuromodulation targets were not evaluated.
Every one of these is stated in the preprint. We would rather you heard them from us first.
