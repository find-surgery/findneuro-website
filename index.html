The Platform

CN-Suite™

An integrated clinical decision-support platform that turns raw neuroimaging and electrophysiology data into actionable surgical intelligence -with zero workflow disruption.

Intelligence Layer
Network Analysis
Critical Nodes™

Proprietary ML algorithm that identifies critical network drivers from sEEG recordings - the contacts whose removal is most likely to produce seizure freedom. Uses only standard-of-care data. No new hardware required.

Foundation Layer
Visualization
MMVT

Interactive 3D brain model integrating MRI, CT, and depth electrode data into a single visualization. The default case-presentation interface for surgical conferences. Free to all centers - ensuring surgeon exposure on every case.

How It Works

Data Integration

Product Roadmap

Building the Platform

A phased regulatory and product strategy. Each version expands clinical reach while building on validated foundations.

Q1-Q2 2026
CN-Suite V1 - FDA 510(k)
MMVT + Critical Nodes (Ictal analysis). FDA submission and target clearance. Beachhead launch at 5 partner centers.
Q2 2027
V2 - Interictal Analysis
No seizure wait required. Actionable within hours. Reduces EMU stays from 10-14 days to as little as 2 days. ~$20K per case.
Q4 2027
Surgical Simulation Module
Simulate network response to ablation in silico. Compare strategies before surgery. Diagnose, simulate, and treat in 1-2 days.
Q2 2028
V3 - HD-EEG / MEG Integration
Extends network analysis to non-invasive modalities. Reaches all 260 Level III/IV epilepsy centers in the U.S.
Q4 2028
V4 - Scalp EEG
Critical node analysis from routine scalp EEG via cross-modal knowledge distillation. Expands reach to 500,000+ diagnostic sessions per year.
