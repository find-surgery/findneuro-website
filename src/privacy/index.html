Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This page describes how FIND Surgical Sciences, Inc. ("FIND Neuro", "we") collects and uses information on findneuro.com. The site is a marketing website. It does not handle patient data, medical records, or protected health information.

Information you provide

If you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email, organization, role, and message you choose to share. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. Submissions are delivered through Formspree, which processes form data on our behalf - see the Formspree Privacy Policy.

Information collected automatically

To understand how visitors use the site, we use:

  • Google Analytics 4 - aggregated, IP-anonymized usage statistics. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag - to measure the performance of any LinkedIn campaigns we run. See the LinkedIn Privacy Policy.

These services may set cookies on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some site features may not work without them.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested materials (e.g. a demo or investor deck).
  • To understand which content visitors find useful so we can improve the site.
  • To measure the effectiveness of any outreach or advertising.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above (Formspree, Google, LinkedIn) under their respective terms, or as required by law.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable follow-up. Analytics data is retained per the provider's standard retention windows.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us by emailing info@findneuro.com. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the browser opt-out add-on.

Security

We rely on TLS for all traffic to findneuro.com and on industry-standard practices from our service providers. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
