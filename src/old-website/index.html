About FIND Neuro

A pioneering platform aimed at improving surgical outcomes for drug-resistant epilepsy patients.

We're changing the approach to epilepsy surgery - reducing risks, and improving the chances of seizure freedom.

By taking a complete look at the network of brain areas involved in seizures, our platform allows for surgeries that are more focused and less invasive.

We pinpoint the most crucial nodes in the seizure network. This allows medical teams to choose surgical or neuromodulation strategies that promise higher success rates.

When a center adopts FIND Neuro, the decision to pursue epilepsy surgery becomes far less daunting - and far more reassuring.

Alice's Story

At six years old, Alice experienced up to 50 seizures daily. Traditional treatments offered only 20-50% success rates.

Using FIND's network analysis, clinicians identified precise surgical targets. Today, Alice is seizure-free.
