Surgical Simulation Deep Dive

How It Works

The Surgical Simulation module uses the sEEG electrodes already implanted in the patient. Nothing additional is needed. It mimics Responsive Neurostimulation (RNS) by temporarily inhibiting a target region through the existing sEEG contacts while continuing to record from all other electrodes simultaneously.

The CN algorithm then analyzes the ongoing recordings to assess the network-level effect of the inhibition in real time: how does the epileptogenic network change when that specific region is suppressed?

Clinical Value

This gives clinicians a virtual "test drive" of ablation strategies before any irreversible procedure. They can quantitatively compare different target combinations, for example "Target A alone" vs. "Target A plus B," and evaluate the predicted impact on seizure freedom.

This supports multidisciplinary consensus, converts uncertain "maybe" cases into confident "yes" cases, and helps clinicians spare the tissue they want to preserve: language, motor, and vision networks.

Predictive validation before irreversible intervention. Clinicians can optimize strategy, not just pick a target.

From Niche Technique to Standard of Care

Today, sEEG ablation outcomes are limited (~15 to 25% seizure freedom) due to imprecise targeting. FIND's combination of interictal critical-node identification and surgical simulation provides the missing piece: confident, validated targets delivered within a day. This transforms sEEG ablation from a palliative "last resort" into a scalable, reproducible curative pathway, reducing length of stay, eliminating the need for open surgery, and fundamentally improving the patient experience.
