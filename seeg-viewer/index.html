Data Pipeline
From hospital to cloud, automatically.
01
On-prem compressor

Sits behind the hospital firewall, compresses raw sEEG losslessly. No PHI leaves the network unencrypted.

02
AWS uploader

HIPAA-compliant, end-to-end encrypted transfer to the FIND Neuro cloud. Resumable across unstable links.

03
Annotated & ready

Recordings appear in the viewer pre-tagged with seizure events and sleep stages, ready for review and Critical Nodes analysis.

The viewer is the adoption hook. The pipeline is how the data starts flowing. Together, they unlock everything Critical Nodes can do.
